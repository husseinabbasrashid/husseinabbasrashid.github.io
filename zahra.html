Chapter three · From my heart

Some things deserve more than a message. Tap the envelope when you’re ready.

A little something, written just for you.

Zahra, my love,

Today you turn 23, and I wanted to write you something more than a quick “happy birthday,” because you deserve much more than that.

23 years. 8,401 days. 201,624 hours. 12,097,440 minutes. And every one of them was slowly getting the world ready for the day you’d walk into my life and make it so much brighter.

I don’t know how to explain what you mean to me. You are the person I want to tell everything to, the one who makes my bad days easier and my good days better. Your smile is my favorite thing to look at, and your laugh can fix my mood in a second. Being with you feels like home.

I’m proud of who you are and everything you’ve become, and I’m excited for everything you’re still going to achieve. You have so much light in you, and I hope you never doubt it.

On your birthday, I wish you:

  • A year full of joy and laughter
  • Dreams that finally come true
  • Peace in your heart
  • Every happiness you deserve, and more

Thank you for being you, for being patient with me, and for loving me the way you do. I promise to stand by you, support you, and celebrate you, today and every day after.

I love you, now and always. Happy birthday, Zahra. 🤍
